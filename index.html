About the Farm

More Than a Farm —
A Commitment to Quality

For over a decade our family has raised cattle the honest way — slow, attentive and rooted in traditional Pakistani farming. Every animal on our farm is treated as an investment in your trust.

  • Healthy LivestockRegular vet checks, vaccination and deworming schedules.
  • Proper NutritionGreen fodder, balanced feed and clean water twice daily.
  • Clean EnvironmentSheds washed and disinfected every single day.
  • Responsible Animal CareLow-stress handling and dedicated daily attention.
  • Quality BreedingSelected Sahiwal, Cholistani and crossbred lines.
  • Customer TrustHonest weights, fair prices, no surprises.
